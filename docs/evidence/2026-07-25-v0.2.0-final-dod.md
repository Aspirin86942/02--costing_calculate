## Performance conclusions

- M6A `cell_text` comparison borrowing was adopted. SK normalize median
  improved `22.1178%`; full wall median improved `1.1771%`; PWS and output
  stayed within gates; the real oracle had zero mismatches.
- M6B compression Levels 3 and 2 were rejected. They improved wall/save time
  but exceeded the SK output-size limit by `3,869,470` and `8,286,515` bytes.
  Level 5 remains the frozen production setting.
- Retained Level 5 SK output was `43,611,044` bytes with median PWS
  `1,462,169,600` bytes, below the `48,658,823` byte and 2 GiB limits.
- The final GB regression was `1.8824031s`, `357,580,800` bytes PWS, and
  `3,808,077` output bytes, all below the frozen GB gates.
- OpenPyXL, desktop Excel, and the repository comparator opened/validated the
  final and compression-candidate workbooks.

Detailed protocols and raw-result boundaries are recorded in the M6A and M6B
evidence documents.
